What each one means

The Description column from the slide, by row number.

  1. Ability to specify format when placing request. Reported by Clermont. Requested 3 items for the same ISBN and got 3 different formats.
  2. Titles missing in Vega. Some titles sent by the GTI get rolled up with an eBook in the CDI, and as such get filtered out of the results in Vega.
  3. Availability display in Vega CRI. With INN-Reach, holdings and availability information are displayed in Encore and WebPAC.
  4. Due dates include hold shelf and shipping. Due dates are calculated by Rapido when the lender ships the item and include shipping and hold shelf time. Patrons can check out two items on the same day with different due dates.
  5. Request multiple items on same title. Possible for staff in Rapido; not in Vega
  6. Expired requests. The expiry time pod setting determines how many days a request remains with a lender before automatically moving to a new lender. Rapido is not notifying the current lender before moving the request to a new lender. Results in multiple items shipped for the same request. Workaround is for lending site staff to cancel a request they cannot fill.
  7. Suppress from participation. Ability to not lend items which are lendable in the ILS but not in RS (for example new material)
  8. Suppress from display. Libraries would like to suppress in Rapido & Vega groups of records which aren't suppressed in the ILS
  9. Vega Custom URL. Customer could specify a custom URL for Vega CRI
